Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Name of the Wind part of a series? Will it be finished?

It is the first book in the Kingkiller Chronicle. The second, The Wise Man's Fear, was published in 2011. The third book, currently titled The Doors of Stone, has been delayed for over a decade. Rothfuss has publicly apologized for the delay and stated he is still working on it — but as of 2026, no publication date has been confirmed. If you want a complete story, jump to the "Completed Series" segment above.

Why does The Name of the Wind feel so different from other fantasy epics?

Rothfuss deliberately subverts classic fantasy conventions — Kvothe is a "Mary Sue" in the best sense, but Rothfuss frames the narrative so that we see Kvothe's own self-mythologizing, inviting readers to question how much of what he tells Chronicler is embellished. His prose also deliberately imitates literary fiction rather than pulp fantasy, which is why it attracts readers who don't usually read epic fantasy.

What are the best editions of The Name of the Wind to compare?

The standard hardcover (DAW Books, ISBN 978-0756404740) is the most widely available. The 10th Anniversary Edition added new internal illustrations and maps. Audiobook listeners recommend Nick Podehl's narration on Audible — his voice work for Kvothe is considered iconic among fantasy audio fans.
