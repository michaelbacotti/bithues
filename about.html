About Bithues

Bithues is a book blog about fiction, ideas, and the worlds between the pages. We review novels, curate reading lists, publish original short fiction, and write about the literature that deserves more attention.

Named for the word that lives between "bites" and "muse," Bithues is for readers who want more than a rating and a summary. We care about what a book asks of its readers, what it gets wrong, and what it gets so right it changes the way you see.

What We Cover

Our content falls into three categories:

  • Book Reviews — In-depth looks at fiction worth your time, from debut novels to overlooked gems. We focus on storytelling craft, character depth, and whether a book earns its ending.
  • Articles & Reading Lists — Themed collections, genre guides, and essays about the reading life. Best books of the year, niche recommendations, and the occasional deep dive.
  • Short Stories — Original literary fiction published here first. We believe in the short form and the writers working in it.

The Approach

We don't do star systems as a substitute for analysis. A four-star review explains why four stars — what earns them, what costs a star, and who the book is for. We try to say something true about every book we cover.

Books change how you think. That's the whole point. We write for readers who believe that too.
