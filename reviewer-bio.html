Meet the Reviewers

The people behind the reviews.

Bithues is run by a small, focused team of readers who read widely and write honestly. We're not professional critics — we're people who read because we can't imagine not reading, and who got tired of wasting time on books that weren't worth it.

📖

The Editorial Team

Founding Editors & Lead Reviewers

Bithues was started by readers who wanted a better way to find their next great book. Our editorial team reads across science fiction, fantasy, self-help, narrative nonfiction, literary fiction, and thriller. Every review published on the site reflects the honest opinion of a human editor who read the book in full.

We don't aggregate reviews or pull descriptions from publisher copy. When we recommend a book, it's because one of us read it and thought you'd find it worthwhile.

Contributing Reviewers

Guest Contributors

We occasionally welcome contributing reviewers who bring specific expertise or perspective — a background in science, education, psychology, or a particular genre specialty. All contributed reviews are held to the same editorial standards as our core reviews and go through the same editing process.

Interested in contributing? Get in touch with a short note about your reading background and areas of interest.
