Best Books for Dad Thoughtful gift ideas for every type of dad Father’s Day, Christmas, or just because — finding the right book for Dad can be tricky.

Does he read at all? Does he prefer fiction or nonfiction? Is he into sci-fi or something more practical?

We’ve curated this gift guide based on the books we’ve reviewed. Every pick is one we genuinely recommend.
