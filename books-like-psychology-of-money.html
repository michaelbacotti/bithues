The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel · 2020 · 256 pages

★★★★★ 4.5 312,847 ratings

Timeless lessons on wealth, greed, and happiness. Morgan Housel shares 19 short stories exploring the strange ways people think about money and teaches you how to make better sense of your own financial decisions. The key insight: financial success is not about how smart you are, it's about how you behave.
