Starting point

Foundation

Isaac Asimov · 1942 · 244 pages

★★★★★ 4.4 18,200 ratings

Isaac Asimov's Foundation series is one of the most influential works of science fiction ever written — and its influence on subsequent space opera and political SF is incalculable. The premise — a mathematician who predicts the fall of a galactic empire and engineers a plan to reduce the dark ages from 30,000 years to 1,000 — is a thought experiment about whether history can be mathematicized.
